# HanguStep release notes

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-14), personal release

Korean study app for TOPIK, offline, English and Khmer. Installed with free signing (ADR-013): iPhone via `Tools/install-iphone.sh` (renew weekly), Mac via `Tools/install-mac.sh`.

**Learn**
- 1,730 words (levels 1-4), including 1,500 TOPIK II words chosen by frequency in past exams, English and Khmer meanings, examples.
- 140 grammar patterns with explanations and examples.

**Practise**
- Quizzes by pool (mixed, vocabulary, grammar, listening, mistakes) and level; 3,830 questions.
- Listening: 100 TOPIK II-style questions with bundled recordings; script shown after answering.
- Writing: 30 tasks for questions 51-54 with TOPIK-style character count, drafts, model answers, offline checks and optional Claude feedback (own API key).

**Remember**
- SM-2 spaced repetition, daily review queue, mistakes list, bookmarks and notes.
- Progress dashboard, streak and daily goal.

**Known limits**
- No iCloud sync in free builds; each device keeps its own progress.
- Khmer translations are AI-drafted and not yet reviewed.
- Recordings are synthetic (MeloTTS); practise with official past-paper audio too.
